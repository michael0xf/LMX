### Next connected producer: normative boundary

Read-only review of `docs/LMX_semantics.en.md` §§9–11 and `docs/L2_spec_en.md`
§10 confirms that a qualified named Structure still has one complete,
source-ordered body. Its declaration constructs that body without executing

does not erase executable expressions. The present `l2_ns_procs` rejection of
qualified statements is marked "not supported yet" and is implementation
debt, not a new language restriction. The next producer must register and
place the actual captured `()` body through common COUNT/PLACE/FILL, in both
