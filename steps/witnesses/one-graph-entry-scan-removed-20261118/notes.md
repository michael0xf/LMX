# The body scan at the entry of a call is removed; the modifiers execute in the code of a method

Written 2026-11-18 by Fable (fable_pc), the single writer/build of main. The code, the fixtures, the component builds
and the mutants are by the peer session sonnet under the pipeline token (his label for the slice is S2); the part on
the modifiers in code is the text prepared by the peer session opus, taken verbatim. Fable set the bounds, decided
which captures stay, read the diff, verified the manifest and the components, re-ran the witnesses and ran his own
probes. Base: `65566681`; the commits between it and this one are documents only.

## The norm

The author, 2026-11-18 (`LMX_blog/2026-11-18.md#translator-interpreter-scheme`, `#interpreter-builds-nothing`,
`#one-level-scan-for-namespace`): the interpreter starts on the finished clean graph and builds nothing before the
start; the pass over the whole body at the entry of every call is removed; at the opening of a namespace one pass over
one level remains, for the names visible both ways -- callables and declarations of Structures; a free name is
resolved when it is first read. `#marking-receivers-execute`: the modifiers execute in code as well as in data.

## What changed

- `lmx_graph_run.lm2`: the ten functions of the entry scan are deleted (`lmx_graph_work_scan`, `_scan_body`,
  `_scan_context`, `_scan_operand`, `_scan_send`, `_scan_word`, `_scan_literal`, `lmx_graph_scan_launch`,
  `lmx_graph_work_assignment_contract`, `lmx_graph_work_capture`); `lmx_graph_work_open_parts` makes no pass over the
  body. A new `lmx_graph_input_take` makes the one capture of the source of a free name when a consumer needs the name
  as a place of this activation: a write by a bare name (`lmx_graph_place_name`), the address of a name (`@name`,
  `lmx_graph_eval_value`) and the read of an operand (`lmx_graph_operand_read`). A name with no source makes no row,
  and the answer 3 is left to the reader. The other readers of a value take nothing.
- The same file, the modifiers in code: a reached record of `private`, `required`, `const`, `immutable`,
  `independent` in the code of a method is executed (`lmx_graph_block_marked`); each declaration given to it is
  reached in turn (`lmx_graph_block_declaration`, the former branch of the block moved without change), and the row of
  the place keeps the record of the modifiers it was declared by (`lmx_graph_place_declared`).
- `l2_graph_tool.lm2`, the emitter of the native image: `@name` calls `lmx_graph_input_take` before the resolve; a
  record of a modifier in code is executed -- the branches of declarations are moved without change of text into
  `lm_graph_emit_declaration`, called for every declaration and for every declaration of a record of modifiers.
- `tools/build.lm2`: the list of the exports of `lmx_graph_run`, 73 to 76.
- 16 new fixtures: ten `scan_*`, three `marks_code_dynamic_*`, three `marks_ref_code_*`.

## Open, not in this slice

- The hold of a free name after its first read has neither code nor a witness. Today the source of a free name is
  always a row of the caller's frame, and it does not change during the call, so the entry, the first read and the
  hold cannot be told apart. A witness needs a name whose source is a cell of the graph and a callee launched while
  the frame of that Structure is not active; today no record launches one. The norm stays written.
- A defect of the tool: a name hidden under `private` is distinguishable from an undeclared one. A reference
  declared under `private` in code and read by a callee as `\p` answers 4; an undeclared `\p` answers 3
  (`marks_ref_code_undeclared_read`, green). `marks_ref_code_private` is pinned 3 by the norm and is red.
- The branch of `lmx_graph_block_declaration` that calls `lmx_graph_place_declared` for references is reached by no
  fixture; the same branch of the emitter was removed as a surviving mutant.
- The fixture `callable_part_names`: its comment and expected text predate the merge of `args` into `data`; the pin
  is right. A positive twin with `length(data)` is a separate small task.
- A formal under a modifier does not take a supplied argument; the check of `required`; `predef:` and `include:`
  -- as recorded in the notes of the modifiers slice.
- A lesson of the stand: an interrupted `build-frontend-host` deletes the generated frontend sources of the shared
  stage, and every later `build-runtime` in that stage fails in the headers step with a misleading message; a whole
  `build-frontend-host` restores it.

## What the running program holds after the step

The clean graph and the native methods, as before. Nothing is prepared at the entry of a call: a row for a free name
appears in the frame of the activation only when the name is needed as a place.
