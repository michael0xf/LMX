## The norm

- A place-declaration receiver supplied as an argument in the context of CODE arguments is read as DATA: the
  argument is the declaration itself, and the passing alone runs no initializer
  ([declaration arguments](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#declaration-arguments)).
- Three rows of the table there: the declared name is found in the receiving DATA — the supplied declaration
  replaces that description in the namespace of this call; the name is absent — the whole declaration takes the
  current position as one Structure, and its inner name does not switch the counter of positions off; an outer
  `formal := declaration` chooses the addressee, and the declaration keeps its inner name.
- The required observations A01–A08, A16 and A17
  ([the table](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#declaration-observations)); A09–A15, the execution of a
  kept form in the root of the receiving execution, are part (b) of the step.
- The contracts of the other receivers do not change: `fn` keeps its DATA arguments (Р121).

## What changed

Kernel only: `selfbuild/src/l2src/lmx_graph_run.lm2`, and one condition of the emitter in
`selfbuild/src/l2src/l2_graph_tool.lm2`. No tool source and no frontend source changes.

- **Binding.** In the code of a call a declaration whose name is no formal of the signature is not an unknown
  named actual: it takes the position, and the counter of positions goes on. The binder gets the role of the
  argument context from its caller; the DATA arguments of `fn` and `sub` are bound as before, and an absent name
  there stays a refusal.
- **Reading.** A form is not evaluated when it is supplied. With the name of its formal it is the description of
  that place: the record goes into the row of the formal, which is made from it and evaluated on demand, once in
  the activation. With another name it is one Structure by value at its position, judged by the one admission of
  the formal.
- **A literal form.** The builder holds `int: item 5` among the arguments as the int cell named `item`; at a
  name that is no formal the reader builds its record, the same kind of record a form with a call for its
  initializer is.
- **Selection of the declaration.** A form is data: supplying it runs nothing, so the selection does not judge
  it as an actual with an effect.
- **Native.** The emitter sends a form to the reader of the runtime, as the interpreter reads it; a call that
  carries a description runs the same body by the interpreter, because the entry of a native body receives
  values.
- Two needless `end:` of step 21.5 are removed.

## Debts

- **The entry of a native body** takes values: a call with a description runs the body by the interpreter. The
  lazily made description comes into the entry with the emitter slice of step 21.7.
- **The builder holds a literal form as a cell.** The reader builds the record only for an int cell at a
  position. Forms of cells without a value and of reference cells answer 4; a literal form on the right of an
  outer name arrives as a Structure of one field (`length` 1, where the computed form gives 2); the record costs
  four cells per call. The general solution is the builder holding a declaration written among the arguments of
  a call as a record for every head; it is the first slice of part (b), the rows are pinned with it, and the
  branch of the reader goes away.
- **A matched name with a formal declared of another type** (`g(int: b 5)` at `@: b`): the form is judged as a
  value by the admission of the formal, where the book says the supplied declaration replaces the description.
  Derived anew in the design of step 21.8 (a).
- **The role of the argument context** is given to the binder by its caller. Receivers with CODE arguments take
  the rule in step 21.11, from the contract of the receiver's formal.
- 63 older comments of `selfbuild/` still name task documents; their replacement with book anchors is prepared
  as a mechanical slice.
