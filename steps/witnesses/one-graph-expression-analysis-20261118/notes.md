# One analysis of an expression for the interpreter and the emitter (step a1 of the expression slice)

Written 2026-11-18 by Fable (fable_pc), the single writer/build of main. The code, the component builds and the
mutants are by the peer session sonnet under the pipeline token; the boundary fixtures are by the peer session
haiku. Fable set the bounds and the readings (the executor's answers R97, R99), read the diff, verified the manifest
and the components, re-ran the witnesses on the final chain and ran his own probes. Base: `d4a5f1bb`; the commits
between it and this one are documents only.

## The norm

The author's requirements `docs/new_parts/to_fable_operands_arguments_priorities.md`, revision 3, sections 5 and 6;
the book, `docs/LMX_semantics.ru.md#assignment-inside-expression`; the grammar, `docs/LMX_grammar.ru.md#operators`:
one table of priorities for the analysis of an expression; a value needed by a receiver is obtained by executing
the receiver that produces it, once; a skipped branch of `&&` and `||` is not executed; a value in an expression is
a record «type and value». Step a1 is the first of the chain: the analysis becomes common, the builder of the graph
and the values of all programs stay as they are.

## What changed

- `lmx_graph.lm2`, a new section: the table of operators `lmx_expr_row` (priority and the roles infix, unary, lazy
  as separate values); the analysis of the fields of an expression into a temporary tree by precedence climbing
  (`lmx_expr_tree`), with operands that follow without an operator read as more arguments of the same operator; the
  recognizer `lmx_expr_is_chain` for the shapes the builder makes today; the operations on typed values
  (`lmx_expr_apply`, `lmx_expr_apply_unary`) with one branch, int by int. The tree lives for one evaluation and is
  not kept in the graph.
- `lmx_graph_run.lm2`: `lmx_graph_eval_tree` walks that tree and returns a typed value; a lazy operation asks its
  next argument only while the result is not decided. The two blocks of `lmx_graph_eval_number` that listed the
  thirteen operators and the unary ones are replaced by the call of the analysis; the int reading is its projection.
- `l2_graph_tool.lm2`: the emitter of the native image walks the same tree; for an operation of two arguments the
  emitted text is the former text.
- `tools/build.lm2`: the exports of `lmx_graph` 93 to 101.
- Fixtures: 40 new rows `expr_*` (the rows of this step and `expr_r01`, the six relations at the boundary). With
  this commit the eight pairs re-pinned after the declaration-selection slice enter the tree: five replace
  `declsel_c09`, `declsel_c10`, `declsel_launch_calls`, `declsel_b10`, `declsel_b10direct`, three are new
  (`declsel_c09_data`, `declsel_c09_by_name`, `declsel_fncall_self_measure`); see the correction at the end of the
  notes of that slice.

## Open, not in this step

- The analysis works in full only after the next step: the builder still nests by priority, so the priorities of
  the table, operators with several arguments and the head forms are reached by no program yet.
- The value of a node is a typed record, but every operation is int by int; the fields of an expression are read
  as ints. The places of the other primitives and the operations on them are the steps e3.0-e3.2.
- The comments of the new section are about 190 characters wide and are to be wrapped at 120 with the next step;
  a typed value is copied by `memcpy`.
- A hole of the row check, found by opus while this step was built: for a row pinned to a refusal `graph-check`
  compares only the interpreter's answer and does not compare the printed graph with the expected file. It is fixed
  by a separate small slice before the next step of this chain (the executor's answers, R104).
