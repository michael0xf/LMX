# The modifiers take several arguments: the data of a call

Written 2026-11-18 by Fable (fable_pc), the single writer/build of main. The code, the fixtures, the component builds
and the mutants are by the peer session opus under the pipeline token (his label for the slice is M1). Fable set the
bounds, read the diff, verified the manifest and the components, re-ran the witnesses and ran his own probes. Base:
`39d04d09`; the commits between it and this one are documents only.

## The norm

The author, 2026-11-18 (`LMX_blog/2026-11-18.md#marking-receivers-execute`, `#two-kinds-of-receivers`,
`#arguments-by-number-and-form`): the receivers `private`, `required`, `const`, `immutable` and `independent` are
executed in the direct pass over data; the variables given to them are root variables; each takes several arguments,
and the arguments are what the record holds by the common rules of the parse. The book:
#data-root-declaration-execution, #qualification, #private-binding.

## What changed

- `lmx_graph.lm2`: `required` is one of the modifiers; new readers of the declarations under a field, depth first in
  written order (`lmx_graph_declared_count`, `lmx_graph_declared_at`) and of the question whether a modifier of a given
  name stands above the declaration of a name (`lmx_graph_record_mark`). They read the record as written each time and
  store nothing.
- `lmx_checks.lm2`: the new check `lmx_check_place_writable` -- a place declared under `const` keeps its binding.
- `lmx_graph_run.lm2`: when the data of a call is prepared (`lmx_graph_work_open_parts`; no new pass) each declaration
  given to the modifiers gets its own working row, a root variable of that data; the row table grows when full;
  `private` hides each such declaration from a callee's dynamic lookup; an assignment by bare name to a declaration
  under `const` answers 4.
- `tools/build.lm2`: the lists of the exports of the two kernel modules.
- 24 new fixtures `marks_*`. The emitter, the builder and every path reader are not changed.

The record of a modifier lies in the graph as written. A chain with an argument that is a Structure keeps its present
reading until the slice that migrates such records.

## Open, not in this slice

- A record of a modifier in the code of a method: refused 3 by the entry scan (above). Opus's draft of the code part
  is an appendix to the slice that removes the scan, out of tree.
- A formal under a modifier does not take a supplied argument: `fn: twice (const: int: a 1) int`, `twice(21)` answers
  5 from the binder, and two declarations under one modifier in a signature are refused at build (5). The norm: the
  formals of a declaration include the declarations under its modifiers. It goes into the slice of declaration
  selection, where the binder is rewritten.
- The check of `required`, several arguments of `predef:` and `include:`, and the protection given by `immutable`
  and `independent` are further parts of the modifiers work.

## What the running program holds after the step

The clean graph and the native methods, as before. Nothing is stored about a modifier: whether a declaration stands
under `private` or `const` is read from the record as written each time it is asked.
