## The norm

- The callee C is executed and its actual parent supplies the DATA; a call cannot supply a replacement parent,
  and a reference to C does not reparent it ([call receivers](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#call-receivers)).
- The order of filling an input ([binding places](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#binding-places)): an
  actual; without it the own ready value, which a local of the caller cannot replace; an input with no
  initializer may be filled from the same-named place of the caller
  ([autofill](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#binding-autofill)).
- A bare write goes to the own place of the activation and changes no source declaration
  ([the call frame](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#call-frame-20261115)).
- `return` describes a result and is no input; a nested Structure is not automatically a formal
  ([the signature](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#fn-signature)).
- The special meaning of the name `data` is withdrawn: a declared `data` is an ordinary name
  ([what is withdrawn](../../../docs/spec/LMX_semantics.en.md#removed-builtins)).

## What changed

One kernel source, `selfbuild/src/l2src/lmx_graph_run.lm2`.

- `C()`, `call(C)`, `call: C` and `call(code := C)` run C over its physical parent; before the slice they ran C
  over itself, so a name of the parent was read in the place of the caller.
- A structural launch — the body is C and the data is not C: the parent, or the data given by `S(D)` or
  `call(D; C)` — opens a row for each input of its data only. An input is a place declaration. Structures,
  callables and `return` open no row and stay visible by name.
- A Structure written inside the declaration of an `fn:` or `sub:` and an object of the FN pool keep the reading
  over themselves until their programs are translated; the former path leaves with slice K6.
- R0 is not touched: R0 without DATA is slice K1b.
- One program is translated: `deferred_reference_places` writes `Self` where it wrote the reserved `data`
  (three lines of the program and three of its expected dump); both kernels read it alike.

## Two simplifications at the acceptance

- The helper's module tested, when it opened the rows, that the data is the parent of C. His own mutant without
  the test survived its rows and six probes: nothing observes it. By the norm the inputs of a call are the place
  declarations of its data, whichever the data is, so the test is removed; that also removes a second walk over
  the owners of C per call.
- A test for a marked group inside the question whether a field is an input could not be reached and is removed.

## Counters per iteration (the kernel threshold is 1 %)

Measured by the helper on his module, before the two simplifications, which only remove calls.

- A loop without calls, a call of an `fn` and a call with an effect: every counter is equal.

  which the norm asks for.

## Mutants

- The one-argument forms leave the data to the launch (C over itself again): killed by five of the new rows.
- A declared `data` read as the reserved name: killed by `sc_declared_data_ordinary`.
- A Structure written inside an `fn:` or `sub:` runs over its parent too: killed by five rows of the base
  (`declsel_c09`, `declsel_c10`, `declsel_launch_calls`, `head_structures`, `literal_line_count`).
- A row for every field of the data: equal by result, told apart by the counter of rows.
- The formal view also for a launch over given data: equal by observation; it became the code.

## Debts

- **Autofill from a written place that was declared without a value** — the repair and its row go with slice K1b.
- **The cost of the entry.** A structural call opens a row for every place of its data at every call, and the
  body usually reads one or two. For an input with its own ready value the row can be opened at the first use,
  because source declarations do not change during a run; the autofill of an input without a value is taken at
  the entry by the norm. Slice K2 decides it by a counter probe.
- **Actuals by position over given data** (`call(A C)`) come with slice K2; the ordinal of an input is not
  observed before it.
- One reference to a task document is left in `tools/build.lm2`, inside a string of a generated header: it goes
  with a slice that rebuilds the tool. Bare section numbers without the name of their document are left for a
  separate review.
